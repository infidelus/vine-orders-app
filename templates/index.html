Add a New Order


Browser button: add items straight from the Amazon page

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar: Add to Vine Orders

Then, when you're looking at a product on Amazon, click it in your bookmarks bar. This app opens in a new tab with the description and price already filled in. Because it reads the page you're already looking at, Amazon's "are you a robot?" checks don't get in the way. It's much more reliable than the Find button.

If you change the address you use to reach this app, drag the button across again.
